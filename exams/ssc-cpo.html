SSC CPO General Knowledge Questions and Practice

CPO Paper 1 has 50 General Knowledge and General Awareness questions — double the GK load of CGL Tier 1 — which makes this section unusually important for the final score.

Paper 1 is 200 questions and 200 marks in two hours, with a penalty of 0.25 marks per wrong answer. Paper 2 is an English language paper. Confirm the current scheme in the official CPO notification.

How to prepare SSC CPO GK

  • Fifty GK questions means breadth matters: cover all ten subjects instead of perfecting one.
  • The 0.25 penalty is mild, so a reasoned attempt on almost every question is usually better than leaving blanks.
  • History, polity and static GK carry the most weight in CPO shifts; keep them on a fast revision cycle.
  • Use two-hour full-length practice so that your GK block does not eat time from reasoning and maths.

FAQs about SSC CPO General Awareness

How many GK questions are asked in SSC CPO?

Paper 1 contains 50 General Knowledge and General Awareness questions of one mark each, out of a total of 200 questions.

What is the negative marking in CPO?

Paper 1 deducts 0.25 marks for each wrong answer, which is lower than CGL and CHSL. Check the notification for the year you are applying.
