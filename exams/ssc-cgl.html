SSC CGL GK Questions and General Awareness Practice

General Awareness is 25 questions and 50 marks in CGL Tier 1, and 25 questions and 75 marks in the Tier 2 General Awareness module. It is the fastest section to attempt if your recall is trained.

Tier 1 is 100 questions and 200 marks in 60 minutes, with a penalty of 0.50 marks for each wrong answer. Tier 2 Paper 1 Section 2 Module 2 is General Awareness with 25 questions carrying 3 marks each, and the Tier 2 penalty is 1 mark per wrong answer. Always confirm the current scheme in the official SSC notification.

How to prepare SSC CGL GK

  • Aim for 20 or more correct answers in the 25 Tier 1 General Awareness questions; that alone is 40 marks.
  • Attempt every question you can reason towards — with 0.50 negative marking, an educated elimination is still worth it.
  • Because Tier 2 gives 3 marks per General Awareness question, the same syllabus pays three times more there. Deep recall beats guesswork.
  • Rotate subjects weekly: polity and history in one cycle, geography and economy in the next, science and static GK after that.

FAQs about SSC CGL General Awareness

How many GK questions are there in SSC CGL?

Tier 1 has 25 General Awareness questions of 2 marks each. Tier 2 Paper 1 has a General Awareness module of 25 questions of 3 marks each. The syllabus for both is the same broad GK and GS syllabus.

What is a good attempt in the CGL General Awareness section?

Anything above 20 correct out of 25 is strong. Since the section is not sectional-timed separately in Tier 1, keep it for the middle of your attempt when your attention is still fresh.
