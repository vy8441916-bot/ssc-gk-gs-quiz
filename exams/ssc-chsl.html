SSC CHSL GK Questions and General Awareness Practice

CHSL Tier 1 gives General Awareness 25 questions and 50 marks out of a total of 200, which makes it a genuine scoring opportunity for candidates who revise static GK and science well.

Tier 1 is 100 questions and 200 marks in 60 minutes with a penalty of 0.50 marks per wrong answer. Tier 2 also includes a General Awareness module; confirm the current module structure and marks in the official CHSL notification before you plan your attempt.

How to prepare SSC CHSL GK

  • CHSL General Awareness leans heavily on static GK, science and one-line factual questions, so daily short practice pays off more than long reading.
  • Learn the standard static GK lists — awards, books, dances, important days and organisations — because they repeat across shifts.
  • Keep a small notebook of questions you got wrong; revise it before every mock test.
  • With 0.50 negative marking, skip only the questions where all four options look equally unfamiliar.

FAQs about SSC CHSL General Awareness

Is the CHSL General Awareness syllabus different from CGL?

The syllabus is broadly the same GK and GS content. CHSL questions tend to be slightly more direct and fact-based, while CGL occasionally asks application-style questions.

How much time should I give to GK for CHSL?

About 20 to 30 minutes of daily practice is enough if it is consistent. Attempt one 20-question mixed set every day and review the explanations.
