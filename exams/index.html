SSC Exam-Wise GK and GS Preparation

The GK syllabus is shared across SSC exams, but the weight it carries is not. These pages set out the General Awareness pattern for each exam and give you a practice set shaped around it.

SSC CGL Combined Graduate Level Examination

General Awareness is 25 questions and 50 marks in CGL Tier 1, and 25 questions and 75 marks in the Tier 2 General Awareness module. It is the fastest section to attempt if your recall is trained.

Pattern & practice →

SSC CHSL Combined Higher Secondary Level Examination

CHSL Tier 1 gives General Awareness 25 questions and 50 marks out of a total of 200, which makes it a genuine scoring opportunity for candidates who revise static GK and science well.

Pattern & practice →

SSC CPO Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police and Central Armed Police Forces

CPO Paper 1 has 50 General Knowledge and General Awareness questions — double the GK load of CGL Tier 1 — which makes this section unusually important for the final score.

Pattern & practice →

SSC MTS Multi-Tasking Staff Examination

In the current MTS pattern the General Awareness paper is a full session of 25 questions carrying 3 marks each, so each correct answer is worth three times a Session 1 question.
