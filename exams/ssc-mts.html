SSC MTS General Awareness Questions and Practice

In the current MTS pattern the General Awareness paper is a full session of 25 questions carrying 3 marks each, so each correct answer is worth three times a Session 1 question.

SSC MTS exam pattern for the General Awareness paper

SSC MTS — question distribution
SectionQuestionsMarks
Session 1 — Numerical and Mathematical Ability20 questions60 marks
Session 1 — Reasoning Ability and Problem Solving20 questions60 marks
Session 2 — General Awareness25 questions75 marks
Session 2 — English Language and Comprehension25 questions75 marks

The two sessions together are 90 questions and 270 marks. Negative marking applies as per the official notification, so read the current notice before deciding how freely to attempt. SSC has revised MTS patterns in the past, so always check the notification for your cycle.

How to prepare SSC MTS GK

  • General Awareness questions in MTS are direct and often repeat from static GK and basic science lists.
  • Because each question is worth 3 marks, even a modest improvement in GK recall moves your total significantly.
  • Prepare the standard lists first: national symbols, important days, dances, awards, books and authors.
  • Attempt 20-question sets daily and read the explanation even when you answer correctly.

FAQs about SSC MTS General Awareness

How many General Awareness questions are there in SSC MTS?

In the current two-session pattern, Session 2 has 25 General Awareness questions of 3 marks each, for a total of 75 marks.

Is MTS GK easier than CGL GK?

MTS questions are usually more direct and drawn from standard static GK and basic science. The syllabus is similar but the framing is simpler.
