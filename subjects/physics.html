Physics MCQs for SSC General Awareness

Physics in SSC papers is applied and everyday: why a thermometer works, what a decibel measures, which mirror is used in a vehicle, what the unit of a quantity is.

1,119 Physics questions in the bank · answers and explanations included

The reliable topics are measurement and SI units, motion and force, work, energy and power, pressure and floating bodies, heat and temperature including specific and latent heat, light with mirrors, lenses and dispersion, sound with frequency, pitch and the decibel scale, and electricity with current, resistance, Ohm's law and household circuits.

Magnetism and electromagnetism, simple machines, and modern physics basics such as radioactivity, X-rays and semiconductors also appear. Many questions ask for the unit of a physical quantity or the instrument used to measure something, so a unit table and an instrument table are worth preparing early.

How Physics is asked in SSC exams

Physics questions usually form a part of the general science block in the General Awareness section of SSC CGL, CHSL, CPO and MTS papers, with a preference for units, instruments and everyday applications.

Q1. The splitting of white light into its components is due to:
  1. reflection
  2. refraction
  3. transmission
  4. dispersion Correct answer

Explanation: Dispersion is used to separate a beam of white light into its constituent spectrum of colours. This phenomenon leads to rainbow.

Q2. Least audible sound for most of the human ear is:
  1. 10.0 μ bar
  2. 0.0002 μ bar Correct answer
  3. 0.005 μ bar
  4. 5.0 μ bar

Q3. Total internal reflection occurs when light travels from a:
  1. any medium at any angle
  2. rarer to denser medium
  3. denser to denser medium
  4. denser to a rarer medium beyond the critical angle Correct answer

Explanation: TIR needs a denser-to-rarer transition and angle beyond the critical angle.

Q4. Hypermetropia or long sight can be corrected by using:
  1. Bifocal lenses
  2. Cylindrical lenses
  3. Concave lenses
  4. Convex lenses Correct answer

Explanation: It can be corrected with convex lenses in eyeglasses or contact lenses. Convex lenses have a positive dioptric value, which causes the light to focus closer than its normal range.

Q5. Fleming’s right hand rule is used to find the direction of the:
  1. alternate current
  2. direct current
  3. induced current Correct answer
  4. actual current

Explanation: Flemings right hand rule shows the direction of induced current when a conductor moves in a magnetic field. The right hand is held with the thumb, first finger and second finger mutually.

Q6. When a body falls freely, its potential energy:
  1. becomes zero immediately
  2. increases
  3. decreases Correct answer
  4. remains constant

Explanation: As height decreases, potential energy decreases while kinetic energy increases.

FAQs about Physics for SSC exams

Do I need to solve numericals for SSC physics?

Very few. SSC General Awareness physics is almost entirely conceptual and factual, so focus on definitions, units, instruments and applications rather than calculations.

How many physics questions appear in SSC CGL?

There is no fixed number, since general science is mixed into the 25 General Awareness questions. Expect a small handful per shift, with more in science-heavy sets.
