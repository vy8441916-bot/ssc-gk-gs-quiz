Indian Polity MCQs and Constitution Questions

Polity is the most predictable part of the SSC General Awareness paper. Articles, amendments, schedules and constitutional bodies repeat in almost every shift, so accuracy here converts directly into marks.

2,773 Indian Polity questions in the bank · answers and explanations included

Most polity questions ask for a specific fact attached to a specific provision: which article abolishes untouchability, which schedule deals with tribal areas, which amendment added a language, who administers the oath to the President. Because the Constitution is a fixed document, these questions have exactly one correct answer and can be learned with confidence.

The topics that appear most often are the sources of the Constitution, the Preamble, fundamental rights and duties, the Directive Principles, the structure of Parliament and the legislative process, the President, Prime Minister and Council of Ministers, the Supreme Court and High Courts, panchayati raj under the 73rd and 74th Amendments, emergency provisions, and the major constitutional bodies such as the Election Commission, CAG, UPSC and Finance Commission.

How Indian Polity is asked in SSC exams

Polity questions appear in every SSC General Awareness paper. In CGL and CHSL Tier 1 they sit inside the 25-question General Awareness section; in CPO Paper 1 that section has 50 questions. MTS Session 2 has 25 General Awareness questions.

Sample Indian Polity questions with answers

Eight questions from this subject, shown with the correct option and explanation so the page is useful even without JavaScript.

Q1. Who certifies a Bill to be a Money Bill in India?
  1. Finance Minister
  2. President
  3. Speaker of the Lok Sabha Correct answer
  4. Prime Minister

Explanation: The Speaker of the Lok Sabha conducts the business in the house. He/she decides whether a bill is a money bill or a non-money bill.

Q2. The 'doctrine of eclipse' in Indian constitutional law relates to:
  1. Judicial appointments
  2. Taxation
  3. Pre-constitutional laws inconsistent with Fundamental Rights Correct answer
  4. Emergency provisions

Explanation: The doctrine of eclipse deals with pre-constitutional laws that become inoperative to the extent they violate Fundamental Rights.

Q3. Which amendment is known for the 'basic structure' limitation on Parliament's amending power being tested?
  1. 42nd Amendment Act, 1976
  2. 44th Amendment Act, 1978
  3. 24th Amendment Act, 1971 Correct answer
  4. 1st Amendment Act, 1951

Explanation: The 24th Amendment Act, 1971 affirmed Parliament's power to amend Fundamental Rights, later limited by the basic structure doctrine.

Q4. The Parliament can legislate on a State List subject if:
  1. The Supreme Court orders
  2. The State Governor approves
  3. The President orders
  4. Two or more States request it or during an emergency Correct answer

Explanation: Under Articles 249 and 252, Parliament can legislate on State List matters in certain circumstances including a Rajya Sabha resolution or request by States.

Q5. The writs for the enforcement of Fundamental Rights are issued by:
  1. The Parliament
  2. The President
  3. The Supreme Court Correct answer
  4. The Election Commission

Explanation: Under the Indian legal system, jurisdiction to issue ‘prerogative writs’ is given to the Supreme Court, and to the High Courts of Judicature of all Indian states. Parts of the law relating.

Q6. There is no provision in the Constitution for the impeachment of the:
  1. Chief Justice of India
  2. Chief Justice of a High Court
  3. Governor Correct answer
  4. Vice President

Explanation: The Governors and Lieutenant-Governors are appointed by the President for a term of 5 years. The term of Governor’s office is normally 5 years but it can be terminated earlier by dismissal.

Q7. Which Article directs the State to make provision for securing just and humane conditions of work and maternity relief?
  1. Article 44
  2. Article 43
  3. Article 41
  4. Article 42 Correct answer

Explanation: Article 42 directs the State to secure just and humane conditions of work and maternity relief.

Q8. Which amendment gave constitutional status to Municipalities?
  1. 73rd Amendment Act, 1992
  2. 65th Amendment Act, 1990
  3. 74th Amendment Act, 1992 Correct answer
  4. 42nd Amendment Act, 1976

Explanation: The 74th Amendment Act, 1992 gave constitutional status to Municipalities.

FAQs about Indian Polity for SSC exams

Which articles are most important for SSC polity?

Articles 12 to 35 (fundamental rights), 36 to 51 (Directive Principles), 52 to 78 (the Union executive), 79 to 122 (Parliament), 124 to 147 (the Supreme Court), 148 to 151 (CAG) and 243 onwards (panchayats and municipalities) cover most of what SSC asks. Learn each article with one or two keywords attached to it.

Are amendments asked in SSC exams?

Yes, and the same few amendments come up repeatedly: the 42nd (mini-Constitution), 44th, 52nd (anti-defection), 61st (voting age), 73rd and 74th (local bodies), 86th (education as a fundamental right), 101st (GST) and 103rd (reservation).
