Chemistry MCQs: Elements, Compounds and Everyday Science

Chemistry questions in SSC papers lean on symbols, formulas, common names and everyday substances — the chemistry of what you already use at home.

1,996 Chemistry questions in the bank · answers and explanations included

The most repeated areas are chemical symbols and Latin names of elements, common names and formulas of everyday compounds such as baking soda, washing soda, plaster of Paris and quicklime, acids and bases with their indicators, the pH scale, the periodic table and its groups, and the properties of metals, non-metals and alloys.

Expect questions on gases and their uses, fuels and combustion, water hardness and purification, fertilisers, soaps and detergents, polymers, and environmental chemistry such as the greenhouse effect and ozone depletion. Atomic structure, valency and types of chemical reactions fill the rest.

How Chemistry is asked in SSC exams

Chemistry sits inside the general science portion of the General Awareness section in SSC CGL, CHSL, CPO and MTS papers, and rewards memory of formulas and common names more than theory.

Explanation: The average kinetic energy of gas molecules is directly proportional to absolute temperature only. This implies that all molecular motion ceases if the temperature is reduced to absolute.

FAQs about Chemistry for SSC exams

Which chemistry topics repeat in SSC exams?

Chemical formulas and common names, acids and bases with indicators, alloys and their composition, and gases with their uses repeat most often. Start there before moving to atomic structure.

Is organic chemistry asked?

Only at a basic level — hydrocarbons, common fuels, alcohols and polymers. You do not need reaction mechanisms for SSC.
