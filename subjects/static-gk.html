Static GK Questions and Answers

Static GK is the catch-all section that decides the difference between a good and a great General Awareness score. It is pure recall, and the same lists repeat year after year.

3,368 Static GK questions in the bank · answers and explanations included

The classic static GK lists are awards and honours with their fields and recent recipients, books and their authors, sports terms and trophies, classical dances and their states, folk dances, festivals and the states they belong to, national symbols, important days, and organisations with their headquarters and heads.

Add to that list firsts in India, superlatives such as the longest river or the largest desert, capitals and currencies of countries, important monuments and their builders, defence exercises and space missions, and the abbreviations that appear in newspaper headlines. Because the scope is wide, the efficient method is to practise in small sets and note every fact you get wrong.

  • Firsts in India, superlatives and world static facts

How Static GK is asked in SSC exams

Static GK contributes a large share of the General Awareness section in every SSC paper, and is often the single biggest sub-topic in CHSL and MTS shifts.

Q1. According to the Ministry of Commerce and Industry (2018-19), which state of India is the largest producer of Rubber?
  1. Maharashtra
  2. Gujarat
  3. Kerala Correct answer
  4. Uttar Pradesh

Explanation: Kerala (490460 tonnes). Top Natural Rubber producing Indian States: Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Tripura, Assam, Meghalaya, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram.

Q2. Devdas Smriti Panthi Dance Award is related to which state?
  1. Rajasthan
  2. Bihar
  3. Jharkhand
  4. Chhattisgarh Correct answer

Explanation: Chhattisgarh. Awards of Chhattisgarh - Guru Ghasidas Samman, chakradhar Samman, Devdas Banjare Memorial Award, Kishore Sahu Samman. Awards of Madhya Pradesh - Kalidas www.ssccglpinnacle.com.

Q3. Vasudeva I who issued coins in gold (dinars and quarter dinars) and copper (single denomination) was a ______ king
  1. Shunga
  2. Vakataka
  3. Shaka
  4. Kushana Correct answer

Explanation: Kushana. Vasudeva I was the last of the "Great Kushans." He ruled in Northern India and Central Asia, where he minted coins in the city of Balkh. The Kushana dynasty was founded by Kujula.

Q4. In the 11th century, the army of which of the following Cholas reached upto Ganga to invade Bengal ?
  1. Rajendra I Correct answer
  2. Rajaraja I
  3. Vijayalaya
  4. Rajadhiraj I

Explanation: Rajendra I (1014 and 1044 CE) - He was also known as Gangaikonda Chola. He defeated Mahipala, the Pala king of Bengal and Bihar.

Q5. Who was the first Indian to take all ten wickets in a Test innings?

Explanation: Anil Kumble took all ten wickets in a Test innings against Pakistan in 1999.

Q7. How many classical dance forms are recognised by the Sangeet Natak Akademi?

FAQs about Static GK for SSC exams

How do I remember static GK for SSC?

Practise in small sets of twenty questions and keep a one-line correction note for every mistake. Repeating the same list three times over a week works better than reading a long PDF once.

Does static GK include current affairs?

The two overlap where a static fact is tied to a recent event, such as a new award recipient. Learn the permanent facts first and keep current updates short.
