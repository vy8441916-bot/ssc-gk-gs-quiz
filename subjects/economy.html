Indian Economy MCQs for Competitive Exams

Economy questions look difficult but repeat a small set of concepts: who regulates what, which committee recommended which reform, what a monetary aggregate means, and which scheme does what.

2,217 Economy questions in the bank · answers and explanations included

The most frequently tested areas are the Reserve Bank of India and its functions, types of banks and accounts, monetary policy tools such as repo rate, CRR and SLR, inflation and its measurement through the CPI and WPI, national income concepts, taxation including GST, the Union Budget, and the five year plans with their broad themes.

SSC also asks about government schemes and their beneficiaries, financial inclusion, institutions such as NABARD, SIDBI and SEBI, agricultural support prices, poverty and unemployment measures, and economic committees and their chairpersons. Questions on terminology — fiscal deficit, current account, balance of payments, repo, reverse repo — are common because they can be answered in one line.

How Economy is asked in SSC exams

Economy questions are usually 2 to 5 per General Awareness section and lean towards definitions, institutions and schemes rather than calculations. In CPO Paper 1 the larger General Awareness section gives more room for them.

Q1. The 'Service Area Approach' in rural banking assigns:
  1. Specific taxes to banks
  2. Specific loans to farmers
  3. Specific deposits to post offices
  4. Specific villages to bank branches Correct answer

Explanation: The Service Area Approach assigns villages to specific bank branches for planned credit.

Q2. The Planning Commission of India was constituted in the year:
  1. 1942

Explanation: The Planning Commission is an institution in the Government of India, which formulates India’s Five-Year Plans, among other functions. It was set up on 15 March 1950, with Prime Minister.

FAQs about Economy for SSC exams

Do I need to study economic theory for SSC?

No. SSC asks factual economy questions — what an institution does, what a term means, who chaired a committee. Understanding basic definitions and the roles of RBI, SEBI, NABARD and NITI Aayog is more useful than studying theory.

Are current economic schemes asked?

Yes, flagship central schemes and recent budget highlights do appear. Keep a short running list of schemes with their ministry and main benefit rather than reading long reports.
