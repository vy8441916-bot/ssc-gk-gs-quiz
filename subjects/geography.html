Geography MCQs: India and the World

Geography questions in SSC papers are map-based and memory-based: which river rises where, which pass connects which valleys, which state produces which mineral. A structured revision of physical, Indian and world geography covers almost all of it.

2,757 Geography questions in the bank · answers and explanations included

Indian geography dominates, particularly rivers and their tributaries, mountain ranges and peaks, passes of the Himalaya, plateaus and coastal plains, soil types and their distribution, the monsoon and rainfall patterns, natural vegetation, national parks and wildlife sanctuaries, and mineral belts with their producing states.

World geography contributes fewer but distinctive questions: latitudes and longitudes, the International Date Line, the solar system, layers of the atmosphere, ocean currents, major deserts, straits, canals and the countries they connect. Physical geography concepts such as earthquakes, volcanoes, plate tectonics and the rock cycle are also regularly tested.

What this subject page covers

  • Earth, latitude and longitude, motions and time zones
  • Atmosphere, weather, climate and the Indian monsoon
  • Rivers of India, drainage systems and tributaries
  • Mountains, peaks, passes and plateaus
  • Soils, vegetation, national parks and biosphere reserves
  • Minerals, energy resources and industrial regions
  • Agriculture, crops and irrigation in India
  • World geography: continents, deserts, straits and canals

How Geography is asked in SSC exams

Geography is one of the larger contributors to the SSC General Awareness section and often the easiest to revise because most answers can be fixed with a map. It appears in CGL, CHSL, CPO and MTS papers alike.

Sample Geography questions with answers

Eight questions from this subject, shown with the correct option and explanation so the page is useful even without JavaScript.

Q1. If ex ante aggregate demand and ex ante aggregate supply are plotted together in a graphical diagram, then at which point equilibrium will be reached?
  1. Where ex ante aggregate demand is equal to ex ante aggregate supply Correct answer
  2. Where ex ante aggregate demand is zero
  3. Where ex ante aggregate demand is less than ex ante aggregate supply
  4. Where ex ante aggregate demand is more than ex ante aggregate supply

Explanation: The aggregate demand / aggregate supply model is a model that shows what determines total supply or total demand for the economy and how total demand and total supply interact.

Q2. Where are most of the earth’s active volcanoes concentrated?
  1. Europe
  2. Pacific Ocean Correct answer
  3. Africa
  4. South America

Explanation: The Pacific Ring of Fire is an area where a large number of earthquakes and volcanic eruptions occur in the basin of the Pacific Ocean. In a 40,000 km horseshoe shape, it is associated.

Q3. According to David Ricardo, during a high deficit situation taxation and borrowings are _________ means of spending
  1. prudent
  2. differentiated
  3. equivalent Correct answer
  4. subsidised

Explanation: Equivalent. Ricardian equivalence is an economic theory that says that financing government spending out of current taxes or future taxes (and current deficits) will have equivalent effects.

Q4. Which river separates the Kutch region from the rest of Gujarat?
  1. Rann channels of the Luni region Correct answer
  2. Godavari
  3. Ganga
  4. Yamuna

Explanation: Seasonal channels drain the Kutch region; the Luni is the major river nearby.

Q5. The Dead Sea is located between which two countries?
  1. Israel and Jordan Correct answer
  2. Egypt and Sudan
  3. Iran and Iraq
  4. India and Pakistan

Explanation: The Dead Sea lies between Israel and Jordan and is famous for its high salinity.

Q6. If in the East it is Guhar Moti, in North it is Siachen Glacier, then in South it is:
  1. Pambar
  2. Trivandrum
  3. Indira Point Correct answer
  4. Light Point

Explanation: Guhar Moti Westernmost inhabited village in India and Siachen Glacier is the Northernmost human habitat of India and Indira point is the Southern point of Indian mainland.

Q7. The Caspian Sea is the largest inland water body and is bordered by Iran and:
  1. Egypt
  2. India
  3. China
  4. Russia and Central Asian states Correct answer

Explanation: The Caspian Sea is bordered by Russia, Kazakhstan, Turkmenistan, Iran and Azerbaijan.

Q8. What is the name of the highest peak of Indian Garhwal Himalayas located in Chamoli district of Uttarakhand state with a height of 7,816 metres ?
  1. Trisul
  2. Hardeol
  3. Mukut Parbat
  4. Nanda Devi Correct answer

Explanation: Nanda Devi. It is the second-highest mountain in India, after Kangchenjunga (8598 m), and the highest entirely within the country. It ranks as the 23rd highest peak in the world..

FAQs about Geography for SSC exams

How should I revise Indian rivers for SSC?

Learn each major river with four facts attached: source, mouth, main tributaries and the states it flows through. That single habit answers most river questions, including the ones that ask which tributary joins where.

Is world geography necessary for SSC MTS?

A few questions on continents, deserts, straits and international boundaries do appear. You do not need depth; a one-page revision of world extremes and major straits is usually enough.
