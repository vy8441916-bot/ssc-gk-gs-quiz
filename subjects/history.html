History MCQs for SSC CGL, CHSL, CPO and MTS

SSC history questions are almost always short and factual: who founded a dynasty, which traveller wrote what, which year an Act was passed. Practising many one-line questions builds the recall that the exam actually rewards.

3,773 History questions in the bank · answers and explanations included

The History portion of the General Awareness section rewards precision rather than long essays. Across recent SSC papers the recurring themes are the Indus Valley sites and their excavators, the Mauryan and Gupta administrations, the Delhi Sultanate and Mughal rulers with their capitals and buildings, the Governors-General and Viceroys with the Acts passed during their tenure, and the freedom movement with its sessions, movements and personalities.

Art and culture has become a reliable scoring area as well, especially classical dance forms, musical instruments and their exponents, folk theatre, painting styles and the awards given in these fields. The questions below are drawn from a bank that is organised topic-wise, so you can work through one theme at a time and then take a mixed set.

How History is asked in SSC exams

General Awareness carries 25 questions (50 marks) in SSC CGL Tier 1 and SSC CHSL Tier 1, and 50 questions in SSC CPO Paper 1. History usually contributes a handful of those questions, and often more in CPO and MTS papers.

Sample History questions with answers

Eight questions from this subject, shown with the correct option and explanation so the page is useful even without JavaScript.

Q1. Chinese travellers visited India primarily because:
  1. they were interested in Buddhism Correct answer
  2. they were invited by the Indian kings
  3. they were interested to study Indian culture
  4. they were interested to stay in India

Explanation: After the spread of the Buddhist religion, Chinese travellers came to India in big numbers to collect religious books and to visit holy places of Buddhism. Notable among those travellers.

Q2. Shah Jahan is known by which title for his love of building?
  1. Engineer King Correct answer
  2. Soldier King
  3. Poet King
  4. Philosopher King

Explanation: Shah Jahan is called the Engineer King for his extensive architectural patronage.

Q3. The Daroga system in 1792 was introduced by whom among the following Governor- Generals in India ?
  1. Lord Cornwallis Correct answer
  2. Lord Minto
  3. Lord Mayo
  4. Lord William Bentinck

Explanation: Lord Cornwallis (1786-93): The system of Daroga as part of Police reforms. Police of each district were placed under District Judge. Each district was divided into Thanas/Police circles.

Q4. Who was the first Vijayanagar ruler to wrest the important fort of Goa from the Bahaman’s?
  1. Reva Raya II
  2. Harihara I
  3. Harihara II Correct answer
  4. Bukka I

Explanation: Harihara II (1377– 1406) succeeded in thwarting the Bahrnani-Warangal combine and wrested Belgaum and Goa in the west from the Bahamani kingdom in 1380. He wrested the Konkan from Bahamanis.

Q5. The Battle of Wandiwash was fought between the English and which other European power?
  1. Danish
  2. Portuguese
  3. Dutch
  4. French Correct answer

Explanation: The Battle of Wandiwash (1760) was fought between the English and the French.

Q6. The Civil Disobedience Movement was also known as?
  1. Khilafat
  2. Home Rule
  3. Salt Satyagraha Correct answer
  4. Quit India

Explanation: The Civil Disobedience Movement was also known as the Salt Satyagraha.

Q7. With whose participation did the Anushilan group join forces when the Bengal Congress broke up into two factions after the death of CR Das?
  1. Ganesh Ghosh
  2. Surya Sen
  3. JM Sengupta Correct answer
  4. Subhash Bose

Explanation: J.M. Sengupta. After the death of Chittaranjan Das (C.R. Das) in 1925, the Bengal Provincial Congress Committee split into two factions — the moderates and the radicals. The radical faction.

Q8. Ashoka's inscriptions are found on all of the following EXCEPT:
  1. Rocks
  2. Coins of the Gupta period Correct answer
  3. Pillars
  4. Caves

Explanation: Ashoka's edicts are found on rocks, pillars and cave walls, not on Gupta coins.

FAQs about History for SSC exams

How many History questions come in SSC CGL?

SSC does not publish a fixed subject split for General Awareness. In CGL Tier 1 there are 25 General Awareness questions covering history, polity, geography, economy, science and static GK together, so the number of pure History questions changes from shift to shift. A steady practice habit matters more than guessing the exact count.

Is NCERT enough for SSC History?

NCERT Class 6 to 10 history covers most of the ancient and medieval syllabus well. For modern India and art and culture, add one good SSC-focused revision book and keep practising previous-year questions, because SSC repeats factual patterns such as 'who wrote this book' or 'which Viceroy passed this Act'.
