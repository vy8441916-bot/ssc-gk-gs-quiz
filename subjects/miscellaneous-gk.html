Miscellaneous GK and Computer Knowledge MCQs

This section collects the questions that do not fit neatly elsewhere: computer fundamentals, government schemes, abbreviations, defence and space facts, and mixed general awareness.

2,465 Miscellaneous GK questions in the bank · answers and explanations included

Computer knowledge is a dependable scoring block, especially for CGL Tier 2 and CHSL, and it also appears in General Awareness papers. Expect input and output devices, memory and storage units, operating system basics, MS Word, Excel and PowerPoint features, keyboard shortcuts, internet and email concepts, networking devices, and basic cyber security terms such as virus, worm, Trojan and phishing.

The rest of the section is genuinely miscellaneous: government schemes and their ministries, abbreviations used in administration and banking, defence exercises and missiles, space missions and satellites, important committees, and general awareness questions that mix two or three subjects. Practising mixed sets here trains you to switch context quickly, which is exactly what the exam demands.

How Miscellaneous GK is asked in SSC exams

Computer knowledge is a separate qualifying module in SSC CGL Tier 2 and appears in CHSL Tier 2, while mixed miscellaneous questions appear in the General Awareness section of all four exams.

Sample Miscellaneous GK questions with answers

Eight questions from this subject, shown with the correct option and explanation so the page is useful even without JavaScript.

Q1. The book ‘Truth, Love and A Little Malice’ is written by:
  1. Tarun TeJpal
  2. Vikram Seth
  3. Khushwant Singh Correct answer
  4. Nirad C. Chaudhuri

Explanation: Khushwant Singh is an Indian novelist and journalist. Singh’s weekly column, ‘With Malice towards One and All’, carried by several Indian and information networks can help getting an edge.

Q2. Bhabha Atomic Research Centre is situated in:
  1. Delhi
  2. Mumbai Correct answer
  3. Chennai
  4. Hyderabad

Explanation: The Bhabha Atomic Research Centre (BARC) is India’s premier nuclear research facility based in Trombay, Mumbai. BARC is a multi-disciplinary research centre with extensive infrastructure.

Q3. Which of the following is the standard English keyboard layout?
  1. DVORAK only
  2. ABCXYZ
  3. QWERTY Correct answer
  4. AZERTY only

Explanation: QWERTY, named after the first six letters, is the standard English layout.

Q4. Which of the following circuit is used as ‘Memory device’ in computers?
  1. Rectifier
  2. Flip-Flop
  3. Comparator
  4. Attenuator Correct answer

Explanation: A flip-flop is a circuit that has two stable states and can be used to store state information. The circuit can be made to change state by signals applied to one or more control inputs.

Q5. Identify a structure that plays a role in motility
  1. Pili
  2. Flagella Correct answer
  3. Villi
  4. Fimbria

Explanation: Flagella are long, whip-like structures that protrude from the cell surface of certain bacteria and eukaryotic cells. Flagella are composed of a protein called flagellin and are powered.

Q6. Under the Ayushman Vay Vandana Scheme launched in Delhi, what is the annual health insurance coverage offered to senior citizens ?
  1. ₹5 lakh
  2. ₹7 lakh
  3. ₹10 lakh Correct answer
  4. ₹15 lakh

Explanation: ₹10 lakh. The scheme is exclusively for senior citizens aged 70 or above residing in Delhi. The scheme was jointly launched by Delhi's CM Rekha Gupta and Union Minister Hardeep Singh Puri.

Q7. Whose tenure has been extended as the Chairman of the Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT) ?
  1. Nitin Gupta
  2. Ravi Agrawal Correct answer
  3. Deepak Mohanty
  4. N. Suresh Krishnan

Explanation: Ravi Agrawal. The Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT) is the apex body for direct tax policy in India, operating under the Department of Revenue, Ministry of Finance. Established in 1944.

Q8. Which amongst the following is a book written by Dr Harivansh Rai Bachchan?
  1. Chidambara
  2. Kapalakundala
  3. Kamayani Correct answer
  4. Prateeksha

Explanation: Harivansh Rai Shrivastav alias Bachchan (27 November 1907–18 January 2003), was a noted Indian poet of Chhayavaad literary movement (romantic upsurge) of early 20th century Hindi literature.

FAQs about Miscellaneous GK for SSC exams

Is computer knowledge compulsory in SSC CGL?

In CGL Tier 2, Paper 1 Section 3 Module 1 is a Computer Knowledge Test of 20 questions, and the Data Entry Speed Test is qualifying. Check the current notification for the exact module structure each year.

What kind of mixed questions appear?

Typically one-line questions on schemes, abbreviations, defence exercises or organisations that do not belong to a single academic subject. They are best prepared through repeated mixed practice rather than deep study.
