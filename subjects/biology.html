Biology MCQs: Human Body, Health and Plants

Biology is usually the highest-scoring science area in SSC papers because the questions come from a small set of familiar topics: organs, vitamins, diseases, blood, plants and classification.

2,296 Biology questions in the bank · answers and explanations included

Human biology dominates. Expect questions on the digestive, circulatory, respiratory, nervous and excretory systems; the function of organs and glands; blood groups and components; vitamins, their chemical names and deficiency diseases; hormones and the glands that secrete them; and common diseases with their causative agents.

Plant biology and general life sciences fill the rest: parts of a flower and their functions, photosynthesis, plant hormones, classification of the living world, characteristics of viruses, bacteria and fungi, cell structure and organelles, basic genetics, and the scientists associated with major discoveries. Nutrition, immunity and public health questions also appear regularly.

What this subject page covers

  • Cell structure, organelles and cell division

  • Plant parts, photosynthesis and plant hormones
  • Classification of living organisms, microbes and genetics

How Biology is asked in SSC exams

General science is a large part of the SSC General Awareness section, and biology is usually the biggest slice within it. Expect several biology questions in CGL, CHSL, CPO and MTS papers.

Sample Biology questions with answers

Eight questions from this subject, shown with the correct option and explanation so the page is useful even without JavaScript.

Q1. Which of the following is NOT a component of blood?
  1. Platelets
  2. Bone cells Correct answer
  3. White blood cells
  4. Red blood cells

Explanation: Bone cells are not part of blood; blood contains RBCs, WBCs, platelets and plasma.

Q2. The type of plants that grow in water are called:
  1. Halophytes
  2. Xerophytes
  3. Mesophytes
  4. Hydrophytes Correct answer

Explanation: Hydrophytes are plants adapted to live in water or very wet conditions.

Q3. Deficiency of insulin in the human body causes:
  1. Diabetes mellitus Correct answer
  2. Goitre
  3. Rickets
  4. Anaemia

Explanation: A deficiency of insulin leads to diabetes mellitus, characterised by high blood sugar.

Q4. The scientist who introduced the binomial system of nomenclature was:
  1. Gregor Mendel
  2. Whittaker
  3. Charles Darwin
  4. Carl Linnaeus Correct answer

Explanation: Carl Linnaeus introduced the binomial system of naming organisms with a genus and species name.

Q5. The iron-containing protein that carries oxygen in muscle is:
  1. Albumin
  2. Haemoglobin
  3. Myoglobin Correct answer
  4. Fibrin

Explanation: Myoglobin stores oxygen in muscle cells, similar to haemoglobin in blood.

  2. None of the options
  3. Insect and fungi

Q7. Which Indian state has launched the country’s first Tribal Genome Sequencing Project ?
  1. Madhya Pradesh
  2. Jharkhand
  3. Odisha
  4. Gujarat Correct answer

Explanation: Gujarat. The project aims to improve healthcare in tribal communities through genetic research, enabling early detection of hereditary diseases and personalised medical solutions.

Q8. Hashish is obtained from a plant. From which part of the plant is it obtained?
  1. Leaves
  2. Stem
  3. Exudates from leaves and female inflorescences Correct answer

Explanation: Hashish, often known as ‘hash’, is a cannabis preparation composed of compressed and/or purified preparations of stalked resin glands, called trichomes, collected from the unfertilized buds.

FAQs about Biology for SSC exams

Which biology topics are asked most in SSC?

Human body systems, vitamins and deficiency diseases, blood groups, hormones and their glands, and plant reproduction are the most repeated. A single well-organised revision of these five areas covers a large share of the questions.

Should I memorise scientific names?

Learn the ones attached to commonly asked organisms and diseases — the botanical names of major crops and the causative organisms of malaria, tuberculosis, dengue and typhoid are worth memorising.
