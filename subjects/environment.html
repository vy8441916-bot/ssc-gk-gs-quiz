Environment and Ecology MCQs

Environment questions are a compact, high-yield set: ecology terms, protected areas, pollution and the international conventions India is part of.

890 Environment questions in the bank · answers and explanations included

Ecology basics come first: food chains and webs, trophic levels, producers and decomposers, biogeochemical cycles, ecological pyramids and the difference between habitat, niche and ecosystem. Biodiversity questions ask about hotspots, endemic species, keystone species and the meaning of in-situ and ex-situ conservation.

The second block is India-specific: national parks, wildlife sanctuaries, biosphere reserves and Ramsar sites with their states, tiger and elephant reserves, and projects such as Project Tiger and Project Elephant. The third block covers pollution and climate — air and water pollutants, the greenhouse effect, ozone depletion, the major protocols and conventions, and the environmental laws and bodies that enforce them.

What this subject page covers

  • Ecosystem, food chain, trophic levels and cycles
  • Biodiversity, hotspots and conservation approaches
  • National parks, sanctuaries and biosphere reserves
  • Ramsar sites, wetlands and mangroves
  • Air, water, soil and noise pollution
  • Climate change, greenhouse gases and ozone
  • International conventions, protocols and summits
  • Environmental laws, bodies and Indian schemes

How Environment is asked in SSC exams

Environment questions have grown steadily in SSC papers and appear in the General Awareness section of CGL, CHSL, CPO and MTS. Protected areas and conventions are the most repeated sub-topics.

Sample Environment questions with answers

Eight questions from this subject, shown with the correct option and explanation so the page is useful even without JavaScript.

Q1. Which sector is a major source of greenhouse gas emissions in India?
  1. Rainfall
  2. Sunlight
  3. Wind
  4. Energy and electricity generation Correct answer

Explanation: Energy and electricity generation are major emission sources in India.

Q2. Catalytic converters in vehicles help reduce:
  1. Fuel use only
  2. Speed
  3. Noise only
  4. Harmful exhaust emissions Correct answer

Explanation: Catalytic converters convert harmful exhaust gases into less harmful ones.

Q3. According to the ten per cent law, how much energy is transferred from one trophic level to the next?
  1. About 1 per cent
  2. About 90 per cent
  3. About 50 per cent
  4. About 10 per cent Correct answer

Explanation: Only about 10 per cent of energy is transferred; the rest is lost, mostly as heat.

Q4. The Mahatma Gandhi Marine National Park is located in:
  1. Tamil Nadu
  2. Andaman and Nicobar Islands Correct answer
  3. Lakshadweep
  4. Gujarat

Explanation: The Mahatma Gandhi Marine National Park is in the Andaman and Nicobar Islands.

Q5. In a national park, grazing and human activity are:
  1. Compulsory
  2. Freely allowed
  3. Prohibited Correct answer
  4. Encouraged

Explanation: Grazing and unauthorised human activity are prohibited in national parks.

Q6. Which was the first national park established in India?
  1. Kanha National Park
  2. Gir National Park
  3. Jim Corbett National Park Correct answer
  4. Bandipur National Park

Explanation: Jim Corbett National Park (originally Hailey) was established in 1936, India's first national park.

Q7. The Cartagena Protocol is associated with the Convention on Biological Diversity and deals with:
  1. Mercury
  2. Hazardous waste
  3. Biosafety of genetically modified organisms Correct answer
  4. Migratory species

Explanation: The Cartagena Protocol concerns biosafety of living modified organisms.

Q8. The Kigali Amendment to the Montreal Protocol deals with phasing down:
  1. CFCs
  2. Hydrofluorocarbons Correct answer
  3. Ozone
  4. Methane

Explanation: The Kigali Amendment targets the phase-down of hydrofluorocarbons (HFCs).

FAQs about Environment for SSC exams

Are Ramsar sites asked in SSC exams?

Yes, questions on Ramsar sites and their states, and on the total number of sites, appear regularly. A state-wise list is a quick revision item.

How is environment different from biology in SSC?

Biology covers organisms and their body systems, while environment covers the relationships between organisms and their surroundings, plus pollution and conservation policy. The two overlap in questions on ecosystems and biodiversity.
