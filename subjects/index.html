SSC GK and GS Subjects

The SSC General Awareness syllabus is broad but finite. These ten subject pages split it into practice-sized pieces, each with a short explanation of what the exam actually asks, a set of worked sample questions and a live practice set.

Start with the subject you find weakest. Twenty questions a day across two subjects covers the whole syllabus in a few weeks, and the explanations mean you are revising while you practise.

How to choose a subject

If you have little time before the exam, take Indian Polity and Static GK first — they are the most predictable parts of the paper. If you are starting early, work through History, Geography and Economy in that order, then the three science subjects. Environment and Miscellaneous GK are small and quick to finish last.
